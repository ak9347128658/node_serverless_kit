# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## In short

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- No harassment, insults, sexualized language or imagery, doxxing, or other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

The full text, including the enforcement guidelines (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban), is at <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/> and applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and any other channel run by the project.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer, **Asif Khan**, through a [private security advisory](https://github.com/ak9347128658/node_serverless_kit/security/advisories/new) (it is private to the maintainers, so it works for conduct reports too) or by contacting the maintainer through the profile at <https://github.com/ak9347128658>. All reports are reviewed promptly and handled confidentially.
